Not measured on a Pixel 10. None is attached to the laptop, and the Android
emulator that is (sdk_gphone64_x86_64, Chrome 113) became unreachable when a
VPN client cut TCP over loopback, which is what adb uses.

What is known about the phone path, from the laptop's GPU under Pixel-class
emulation (412x915 at 2.625x, Android UA, this build):
  phone defaults      5.4 ms/frame   418 draws  387k triangles  1x pixels
  every option on     9.0 ms/frame   854 draws  644k triangles  1.5x pixels
A Pixel 10's GPU is several times slower than the laptop's Intel Arc, so
these are floors, not predictions. On the phone itself the first-run tuner
(U13) measures the real frame gap and steps down until the median is under
37 ms, so a slow result adjusts itself; a Pixel 10 number is still owed.
